# D55. The test data of mimetype stays as it is

Status: Decided.

The maintainers decided on 2026-11-15 to leave the test data and the case
names that came from `github.com/gabriel-vasile/mimetype` as they are. This
answers question 5 of `docs/PLAN.md`.

The case names and 16 of the 21 files in `testdata/` came from the tests of
mimetype (D11). The repository adds no copyright notice of mimetype, and the
two case names that cite its issues, "json issue#239" and "jar in zip #639",
stay as they are.
